I trained in hospitality and worked hotel floors before joining my family's trading business, which I ran hands on for eight years: the buying, the invoicing, the collections, the stock counts, the reconciliations, the staff, the 9pm spreadsheet sessions. In 2015 I opened our second branch from a bare warehouse and built the team that still runs it today.

In 2022 I moved to Dubai and did the thing every owner dreams about: I systemised myself out. Installed a manager, built the controls, and have directed the business remotely ever since, while running the operations of a nine unit residential portfolio here.

Handomo started as a tool for my own problem. The checking that used to swallow my afternoons became an AI agent that does it weekly without me. When its first full run caught nine invoices marked paid that customers still owed, each one confirmed by my team, I stopped thinking of it as a tool and started thinking of it as the product.

Handomo is a young company and I won't pretend otherwise. You won't find borrowed logos here, and I won't invent any. What you get instead is a system proven on a real business, an offer that costs nothing to test, and a founder who answers his own email within one working day, wherever in the world you are.
