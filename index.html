Fair questions

The things owners actually ask.

What happens to my data?
The free trial run works from a simple export you send, so nothing connects to your systems at all. For ongoing work, access is limited to the one job we agree, and your data is never shared with third parties or used for anything except your own reports.
What if it makes a mistake?
The system flags items for your decision rather than acting on its own, so a wrong call surfaces to you instead of going out silently. Nothing is sent to your customers or suppliers without your approval. The system points, you decide.
Am I locked into anything?
No. The trial run is free. The pilot is a one time fixed price. The retainer is monthly and you can cancel any month. There is no build fee to recover, so there is no lock in logic anywhere.
Who actually does the work?
The automation does the work. It handles the repetitive sorting, drafting and checking, fast and without getting bored. Anything that needs a judgment call comes to you, not to a black box, and you deal with the founder directly, not an account manager or a junior. We reply within one working day.
Why is this cheaper than hiring an admin?
A full time hire costs a multiple of this once you count salary, benefits and management time. Handomo automates the repetitive part and hands you the decisions that matter, so you pay for outcomes on specific jobs instead of a full seat.
Do I need to change my software?
No. We build around whatever you already use, spreadsheets included. If your current setup is messy, that is normal, and it is exactly the kind of mess this is built to handle.
