Fair questions

The things owners actually ask.

What happens to my data?
The free trial run works from a simple export you send, so nothing connects to your systems at all. For ongoing work, access is limited to the jobs we agree, and your data is never shared with third parties or used for anything except your own reports.
What if it makes a mistake?
The system flags items for your decision rather than acting on its own, so a wrong call surfaces to you instead of going out silently. Nothing is sent to your customers or suppliers without your approval.
How fast can we start?
Days, not weeks. You email us the job you hate, we tell you what export we need, and the free trial run usually lands back with you inside a week. There's no discovery call gauntlet unless you want one.
Am I locked into anything?
No. The trial run is free. The pilot is a one time fixed price. The monthly plan can be cancelled any month. There's no build fee to recover, so there's no lock in logic anywhere.
Who actually does the work?
The automation does the repetitive sorting, drafting and checking, fast and without getting bored. Anything needing a judgment call comes to you, not to a black box. You deal with the engineer who builds your system, and we reply within one working day.
Why is this cheaper than hiring?
A hire costs a multiple of this once you count salary, benefits and management time. Handomo automates the repetitive part and hands you the decisions that matter, so you pay for outcomes on specific jobs instead of a full seat.
Do I need to change my software?
No. We build around whatever you already use, spreadsheets included. If your current setup is messy, that's normal, and it's exactly the kind of mess this is built to handle.
Do you work with businesses outside the UAE?
Yes. Handomo is headquartered in Dubai and works remotely with owner run businesses anywhere in the world. Everything is priced in US dollars and delivered over email and the tools you already use.
